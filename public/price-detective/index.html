How patterns are selected

Local extrema use a 60-minute window on each side; endpoints without full context are excluded. Flat series produce no extrema. Rises and falls compare prices one hour apart, ranked by change; overlapping windows are suppressed. These are descriptive patterns, not anomaly scores.

Sources & method

ENTSO-E Transparency Platform · DE-LU bidding zone · 12–14 September 2026. Captured 10 November 2026. All 288 intervals are complete. No interpolation or synthetic values are used.

  • Energy Prices · TR 12.1.D: Sequence 1 (SDAC), EUR/MWh. Sequence 2 (EXAA) is excluded.
  • Actual Generation per Production Type · TR 16.1.B&C: solar; wind = onshore + offshore. Published actuals can include measured, planning and extrapolated values and can be revised.
  • Total Load · TR 6.1.A&B: Actual Total Load, not the day-ahead forecast.

Generation and load are converted from MW to GW. Residual load is calculated as actual total load − solar − wind, before display rounding. It is not a total supply shortage. Times follow the source’s CET/CEST setting: these September dates use CEST (UTC+2). Prices are shown to two decimals and fundamentals to three.

Interpretation: this is a retrospective comparison, not a causal model of auction prices. Generation/load forecasts, bids, outages, cross-border flows and flexibility would be needed to investigate further.
